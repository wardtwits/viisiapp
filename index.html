What kind of word game is Viisi?

Viisi is a daily anagram word game for iPhone. Each puzzle starts with one shared set of five letters, and your goal is to find five valid words from those letters. It is designed for players who enjoy daily word games, vocabulary puzzles, and Wordle-style challenges but want a slightly deeper format.

Unlike many mobile word games, Viisi is fast to open, works offline, does not require an account, and keeps the experience focused on the puzzle itself. Players can track streaks, unlock hints, and revisit past puzzles in the archive.
