Five letters.
Five words.
One puzzle.

Viisi is a daily anagram word game. Solve one new word puzzle each day by making five valid words from the same five letters. Viisi is a clean, fast Wordle alternative for players who want a deeper daily word challenge.

How to play

Daily Letters

Open Viisi to see today’s letters.

How the puzzle works

One set of letters.
Plenty to discover.

Rearrange all five letters to make a word. Then do it again until you have found five different accepted words. There can be more than five answers, so every solve leaves room for discovery.

  1. 1Study the five daily letters.
  2. 2Rearrange every letter into a word.
  3. 3Find five different words to win.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything you need before your first puzzle.

Is there only one correct set of five words?

No. Any five different accepted words made from the daily letters can solve the puzzle.

When does the daily puzzle change?

A new puzzle starts at 06:00 UTC. The local time depends on your time zone and daylight saving time, and the app shows a countdown to the next puzzle.

Can I play a puzzle I missed?

Yes. Open the Archive to play past puzzles. An archive win does not extend your daily streak.

What is different about Expert Mode?

Hints are disabled, three invalid submissions end the attempt, and a win earns 30 extra points.

What happens if I use a hint?

In Classic Mode, hints unlock after three words. A hint fills in the first two letters of a solution and costs 10 points. You can use up to two.

What happens if I reveal the solution?

The attempt ends without a win, and the reveal cannot be undone. Revealing today’s puzzle also ends the current streak.

Can I play a puzzle again?

Yes. A replay is practice, so it does not change the original result, statistics, or Total Score.
