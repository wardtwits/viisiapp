Privacy Policy for Viisi

Last Updated: October 2026

Overview

Viisi respects your privacy. Viisi is designed to be a simple, offline, privacy-first game experience. Most gameplay happens entirely on your device and does not require creating an account.

However, some app features (such as optional email feedback and sharing achievements) may involve data being processed by your device’s operating system or third-party services.

This Privacy Policy applies to Viisi for iOS. No advertising is shown, and Viisi does not sell your personal information.

Information We Do Not Collect (Core Gameplay)

During normal gameplay, Viisi does not require you to provide personal information and does not require an account. If you are signed in to iCloud, Viisi can sync your game history and badges across your devices with the same Apple ID (see below).

Viisi does not automatically collect the following during normal offline gameplay:

  • Personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or contact details)
  • Precise location data
  • Payment or financial information

All core game logic runs locally on your device.

Optional Features That May Transmit Data

iCloud Sync (Optional)

If iCloud is available on your device, Viisi can sync your game history and badges across devices signed in with the same Apple ID using Apple's iCloud services (CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage). This data is stored in your own iCloud account and handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policies; the developer does not receive or operate a server for it.

Game Center (Optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, Viisi submits leaderboard results (such as your longest daily streak, total wins, hint-free wins, and fastest hint-free solve time) to Apple's Game Center. Game Center is operated by Apple and handled under Apple's privacy policies.

Feedback / Support Email (Optional)

If you choose to send feedback using the in-app email composer, your email will be sent using your configured email account. The email may include:

  • Your email address (as the sender)
  • The message content you type
  • Any attachments you choose to include

Viisi does not automatically read your email address or contacts. You are choosing to send the email.

Sharing (Optional)

If you share a badge/achievement, the content you share (such as an image and a website link) is sent through the sharing method you select (Messages, Mail, etc.). Viisi does not control how the receiving service handles that data.

Third-Party Services

Optional features like email and sharing rely on your device’s built-in system services and whatever external service you choose (e.g., your email provider or messaging app). Viisi does not control how those services handle data you choose to share.

Children’s Privacy

Viisi is suitable for users of all ages. Viisi does not require accounts and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If a child uses the optional email feedback feature, they may transmit personal information via email depending on what they write. Parents/guardians should supervise email usage where appropriate.

Data Security

Because Viisi does not use accounts or a backend server for gameplay data, there is no central database of personal gameplay data stored by the developer.

If you email support, the security of that message is handled by your email provider and the developer’s email provider.

Your Choices

  • You can choose not to use optional sharing or email features.

Changes to This Policy

If Viisi introduces new features that collect or process data (such as accounts or analytics), this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can contact the developer using the in-app feedback option or via the contact method listed on the official Viisi website.

Support@Viisi.app

By using Viisi, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
