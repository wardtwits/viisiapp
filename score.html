01 / The essentials

Find five.
Build your score.

The same formula scores both modes: word points + completion bonuses + Special Word bonuses − hint penalties. Scores never fall below zero.

Viisi scoring formula: points for words, completion, Expert wins, Special Words, and hints
ActionPoints
Each accepted word+20
Finish all five words+20
Win in Expert Mode+30
Each distinct Special Word+25
Each hint used−10

Word points appear as you go. Completion and Expert bonuses are awarded only when you finish all five words. Your mode locks when you start the puzzle; changing settings later does not change its scoring.

Classic win

Classic win: 120points

Five words × 20, plus 20 for finishing. Before Special Word bonuses and hint penalties.

Expert win

Expert win: 150points

The same 120, plus 30 for finishing in Expert. Before Special Word bonuses. Hints are unavailable.

How do Special Words add points?

Some puzzles hide Special Words. Their tiles flash gold when found, and each one adds 25 points on top of the base score.

Each distinct Special Word earns its bonus once. Changing its capitalization or spacing cannot make it count twice.

Hints never prefill a Special Word, including as a fallback. Puzzles with Special Words have enough non-special answers for the hinted rows.

What happens when I use a hint?

Classic hints unlock after three valid words. Each hint reveals the first two letters of the next row and costs 10 points. You can use at most two, for rows four and five, so hints can deduct at most 20 points. Expert Mode has no hints.

Using a hint disqualifies that solve from the Hint-Free badge and hint-free leaderboards. It does not cost you the win, the streak, or a One Shot badge.

What if I reveal the solution or fail in Expert?

The displayed score keeps points for completed words and Special Words, minus hints, without a completion bonus. It never falls below zero.

Those displayed points do not add to Total Score. Revealing or failing makes later attempts practice for that puzzle, even if you switch modes.

Your pace.

Time never changes your points.

Solve Time is recorded separately. A faster or slower solve never adds or removes points.

Why do some score cards show fewer lines?

The Words line always shows 20 points for every completed word, even if you did not finish all five. Solve Bonus appears only on a completion, with an extra Expert Win line for an Expert completion.

Hint and Special Word lines appear only when their counts are above zero. Solve Time appears below the total and is never scored.

The breakdown appears only when the saved details can account for every line and add up to the saved total. Some older results therefore show their score without a breakdown; the app does not invent missing points.

Which wins count toward Total Score?
  • A puzzle must be won on its first finished attempt for its original score to count.
  • Revealing the solution or failing in Expert Mode makes later attempts practice for that puzzle, even if you switch modes.
  • Replays do not change the original result or add to Total Score.
  • Older wins from before the Total Score feature do not receive points retroactively.
What happens when I replay a puzzle?

Your first legitimate completion saves a permanent result: score, mode, hint count, Special Words, invalid guesses, and solve time.

Finish a replay in the same sitting and a temporary card labelled Replayed shows that run. Leave the screen and return, and you see the original saved result again.

Replays never add to Total Score, win count, win rate, streak, or solve times. Resetting a puzzle you never legitimately solved does not turn it into an eligible win, and cannot damage an already saved result.

How are solve times recorded?

The clock runs only while you are on the board. It pauses when you leave the game or put the app in the background, then stops and locks with the score when the fifth word lands.

Times appear as 04m12s, or 42s for a sub-minute solve. Every timed legitimate win contributes to average solve time, separately for Classic and Expert.

Fastest Solve is one lifetime record across both modes and counts hint-free wins only. Beat your previous best and the win screen calls out the new record.

Which achievements appear after a win?
First Perfect Score
Your first win that earns the maximum available on that board: the full base plus every Special Word bonus.
Fastest Solve
A timed, hint-free win quicker than every earlier hint-free win, in either mode.
New Longest Streak
A daily streak that passes your previous personal best.
One Shot
A start-to-finish solve without leaving the board, backgrounding the app, resetting, or revealing. Hints do not disqualify it.
How do badges and leaderboards work?
5 Streak
Five daily puzzles solved on five consecutive days.
25 Wins
Twenty-five lifetime legitimate wins, daily or archive.
Hint-Free
A win with zero hinted rows.
One Shot
A single-session solve with no reset, no reveal, and no leaving the board.
