## What is Viisi?

Viisi is a daily word puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Each day, everyone gets the same set of five letters. The challenge is to rearrange those letters to find **five different valid words**. Each word must use all five letters exactly once. Find five words to solve the puzzle.

A useful short introduction:

> Five letters. Five words. One daily puzzle. Rearrange the letters, discover words, and see how many you can find.

Viisi combines a shared daily challenge with room to explore: there can be more than five accepted answers, and players can solve the puzzle with any five different valid words. Words must be in Viisi's dictionary; proper names and abbreviations are not accepted.

For a simple illustration, the letters **A, E, L, R, T** can make **ALERT**, **ALTER**, and **LATER**. Each uses the same five letters once. This illustrates how rearranging works; the goal is still five different accepted words.

## How to play

1. Open today's puzzle and look at the five letters.
2. Arrange all five letters to make a word and submit it. Each letter must be used exactly once per word.
3. Keep finding different accepted words from those same letters. A word cannot count twice.
4. Find five words to win. After the game, tap a completed word to see its definition.

A playable tutorial introduces the board and lets new players practice without affecting their real results or statistics. It can be replayed from How to Play.

## A new puzzle each day

Everyone receives the same daily puzzle. A new puzzle starts at **06:00 UTC** each day; the local clock time depends on the player's time zone and daylight saving time. The app shows a countdown to the next puzzle.

Missed a day? Visit the Archive to play past puzzles. A first-time archive win counts as a win, but it does not extend the daily streak. Players can also replay a finished puzzle for practice; the original result and statistics stay unchanged.

## Classic and Expert

**Classic Mode** lets players work through the puzzle with hints available after they have found three words. Invalid submissions do not have Expert Mode's three-attempt limit.

**Expert Mode** adds a challenge: hints are unavailable, and three invalid submissions end the attempt. Completing the puzzle in Expert Mode earns an additional 30 points. Choose the mode before starting a puzzle; the mode locks once play begins.

## Help when you're stuck

- **Hint:** In Classic Mode, hints unlock after three words have been found. A hint clears the current entry row and fills in the first two letters of a solution. There can be up to two hints per puzzle, and each hinted row costs 10 points. Hints do not reveal Special Words.
- **Reveal:** Shows the solution and ends the game. This cannot be undone. Revealing today's puzzle also ends the current streak. A reveal is not a win.
- **Reset / replay:** Play a puzzle again for practice. The original score and statistics do not change, and a replay does not add to Total Score.

## Scoring

Players earn points as they find words:

| Action | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Each accepted word | +20 |
| Complete all five words | +20 extra |
| Complete in Expert Mode | +30 extra |
| Find a Special Word | +25 per word |
| Use a hint | −10 per hinted row |

A completed puzzle starts at **120 points in Classic** or **150 points in Expert**, before Special Word bonuses and hint penalties. Some puzzles contain hidden Special Words; finding one earns a bonus and its tiles flash gold. Scores never fall below zero. Points earned in an unsuccessful attempt may still be displayed, but they do not count toward Total Score.

**Total Score** reflects eligible original wins, counted once per puzzle. A puzzle must be won on its first finished attempt to add its original score. Revealing a solution or failing in Expert Mode makes later attempts practice for that puzzle, even if the player switches modes. Replays do not change the original result. Older wins from before the Total Score feature do not receive points retroactively.

### Suggested FAQ

**Is there just one correct set of five words?**  
No. Any five different valid words made from the day's letters can solve the puzzle.

**When does the daily puzzle change?**  
At 06:00 UTC. Your local time for the change depends on your time zone and daylight saving time.

**Can I play a puzzle I missed?**  
Yes. Open the Archive. An archive win does not extend your daily streak.

**What happens if I use a hint?**  
In Classic Mode, after three words, a hint fills in the first two letters of a solution and costs 10 points. You can use up to two.

**What happens if I reveal the solution?**  
The attempt ends without a win, and the reveal cannot be undone. Revealing today's puzzle ends the current streak.

**Can I play a puzzle again?**  
Yes. Replay is practice: it does not change your original result, statistics, or Total Score.

**What is different about Expert Mode?**  
Hints are disabled, three invalid submissions end the game, and a win earns 30 extra points.
