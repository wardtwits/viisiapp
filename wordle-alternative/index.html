Why players try Viisi

Same daily habit, a different puzzle.

🧩 More to solve

Wordle asks you to find one hidden word. Viisi gives you five letters and asks for five different words from them, so you're thinking in anagrams rather than letter positions.

Want it tougher? Expert Mode turns hints off and ends the game after three invalid submissions.

♾️ Play on your schedule

You get one new puzzle a day, plus an archive of every past puzzle. A missed day or a favorite puzzle is always one tap away.

Replays start from a clean board and never change your original score, time, or stats.

🛡️ Quiet and private

On iPhone, Viisi has no ads and no account to create. We don't collect or sell your data, and the game works offline.

📚 Learn as you play

Finding five words from one set of letters introduces words you might not know. After a win, tap any word to see its definition.

How to play

Quick questions

Is Viisi a Wordle clone?

No. Both are daily five-letter word games, but in Viisi you find five different words from one shared set of letters instead of guessing a single hidden word.

Is Viisi free?

Viisi is free to download, and there are no ads on iPhone.

Do I need an account?

No. Viisi works without an account and without a connection.

Can I play on Android?

Viisi is currently available on iPhone, with Android coming soon. In the meantime you can try the web demo in any browser.

What if I get stuck?

In Classic mode, hints unlock after you've found three words. You get two per puzzle, at 10 points each. Expert Mode turns hints off.
