🛡️ Experience Quality: Ad-Free & Private

Viisi is a completely ad-free experience. There are no banners, no pop-ups, no trackers, and no data collection. Your puzzle is yours alone.

Wordle, now owned by The New York Times, collects user data and has introduced premium features behind paywalls. Each puzzle feel burdened by corporate interests. With Viisi, you get a clean, mindful experience that respects your privacy and time.

When you open Viisi, you know exactly what you're getting: a daily puzzle, well-crafted gameplay, and nothing else. No distractions. No algorithms. Just the puzzle.

🧩 Gameplay Depth: 5 Words vs. 1 Guess

Wordle gives you 6 guesses to find one 5-letter word. It's simple but shallow. Viisi challenges you to find five different 5-letter words from a shared set of letters—an entirely different puzzle type.

This isn't just "more Wordle." It's a fundamentally richer experience. You're thinking about anagrams instead of letter positions. You're discovering vocabulary instead of guessing letter combinations. Each word you find opens new possibilities for the next, creating actual strategic depth.

Wordle's appeal faded for many players because the mechanic repeats daily. Viisi stays engaging because the complexity deepens the more you play. You'll find yourself looking for obscure words, learning definitions, and actually expanding your vocabulary.

♾️ Availability & Freedom: Play Anytime, Anywhere

Wordle's "one puzzle per day" model was novel once, but it becomes frustrating when you want more engagement. Viisi lets you play as many daily puzzles as you want, and you can revisit the entire puzzle archive anytime you wish.

This means you're never waiting for tomorrow's puzzle. You're never locked out. You're never at the mercy of Wordle's daily schedule. With Viisi, the game adapts to your life, not the other way around.

Love a particular puzzle? Play it again. Want to catch up on old puzzles? The archive is there. This freedom transforms the game from a daily obligation into a genuine choice—play when you want, as much as you want.

⚖️ Difficulty & Variety: Consistent Challenge

Wordle's difficulty is largely determined by luck. Some days are trivially easy; others feel impossible. Players have complained that daily Wordle puzzles have become either too easy or frustratingly hard, mostly due to the limited word pool.

Viisi maintains a consistent challenge level across all puzzles. Every day is thoughtfully designed to be solvable yet rewarding. You won't feel cheated by an unguessable word or bored by an obvious one.

Additionally, finding five words from one letter set naturally creates vocabulary diversity. You'll encounter words you didn't know existed, look up their definitions, and learn. Wordle's word repetition can feel stale after a few months. Viisi's anagram nature keeps every puzzle feeling fresh.
