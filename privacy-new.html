Privacy Policy for Viisi

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

Español (México)

Overview

Your privacy matters to us.

Viisi is designed to be a simple, enjoyable, privacy-first word game. You can play without creating an account, providing personal information, or enabling iCloud.

Core gameplay takes place on your device, including word validation, scoring, and puzzle progress.

To help improve the game, Viisi collects limited, privacy-preserving gameplay statistics. These statistics help us understand how the game performs, identify difficult puzzles, and improve the experience for everyone.

Viisi does not display advertisements, sell personal information, or track your activity across other apps or websites.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Viisi processes, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Do Not Collect

Viisi does not require or intentionally collect the following information through gameplay analytics:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your Apple ID or Game Center account identifier
  • Precise or approximate geographic location
  • Contacts, photos, or personal files
  • Payment or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Your activity in other apps or websites

Viisi does not use gameplay analytics to create persistent individual player profiles.

We do not sell gameplay information or share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

Anonymous Game Analytics

Viisi collects a limited amount of gameplay information to understand how the game is used and improve its quality.

Information We Measure

Depending on the feature being used, these statistics may include:

Game Activity

  • App launches
  • Daily puzzle starts and completions
  • Classic and Expert mode usage
  • Puzzle reveals
  • Hint usage
  • Bonus words found
  • Puzzle scores and completion times

Game Features

  • Tutorial completion
  • Use of statistics, achievements, and leaderboards
  • Sharing feature usage

Technical Information

  • General device category, such as iPhone or iPad
  • iOS or iPadOS major version
  • Viisi app version
  • General performance and reliability information

Puzzle Difficulty

  • Aggregate completion rates
  • Average solving times
  • Average hints used
  • Reveal rates for individual daily puzzles

We use this information exclusively to understand gameplay, improve difficulty balancing, evaluate features, and maintain application quality.

How We Protect Your Privacy

Viisi's analytics system is designed to minimize the information collected.

  • We do not collect your name or account information for analytics.
  • We do not collect individual word guesses.
  • We do not upload your complete puzzle history for analytics.
  • We do not combine analytics with Game Center profiles or iCloud identities.
  • We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics for targeted advertising.

To avoid counting the same device repeatedly within a day, Viisi may use temporary, randomly derived identifiers that change with each analytics day.

These identifiers are not used to identify you, recognize you across different days, or construct a history of your individual activity. Temporary deduplication information is automatically deleted when no longer needed.

Gameplay statistics are maintained in aggregate form rather than as persistent individual player histories.

Analytics and Internet Connectivity

Viisi remains playable offline.

When internet connectivity is available, limited analytics information may be transmitted securely to the analytics service.

Analytics transmission is separate from gameplay and is not required to complete puzzles, maintain local progress, or use the game's core features.

If analytics cannot be transmitted, gameplay continues normally.

iCloud Synchronization (Optional)

If iCloud is available on your device, Viisi can synchronize your game history, statistics, and achievements across devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

Synchronization uses Apple's iCloud services, including CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage.

Your synchronized gameplay information is stored in your personal iCloud account and processed through Apple's infrastructure.

The developer does not maintain a separate central database containing your personal iCloud game history.

iCloud synchronization is independent of Viisi's aggregate gameplay analytics.

You can manage iCloud access through your device's settings.

Game Center (Optional)

If you use Apple's Game Center, Viisi can submit leaderboard scores and achievements through Apple's services.

Leaderboard information may include:

  • Total wins
  • Lifetime points
  • Daily streak records
  • Hint-free wins
  • Fastest eligible solving times

Game Center is operated by Apple and is subject to Apple's privacy practices.

Viisi does not use your Game Center identity to create an analytics profile.

Feedback and Support (Optional)

If you contact us using the in-app email feature, the information you provide may include:

  • Your email address
  • Your message
  • Any attachments you choose to send

Viisi does not automatically access your contacts or read your email.

Information provided through support communications is used to respond to your questions, investigate problems, or improve the application.

Support communications are handled separately from anonymous gameplay analytics.

Sharing (Optional)

Viisi allows you to share achievements, badges, and game-related content using your device's sharing features.

When you choose to share something, the information is handled by the application or service you select, such as Messages or Mail.

Viisi does not control the privacy practices of those external services.

Third-Party Services

Viisi uses certain Apple services to provide optional functionality, including:

  • Apple iCloud and CloudKit
  • Apple Game Center
  • Apple App Store and diagnostic services
  • Built-in iOS sharing and email features

Viisi's gameplay analytics are designed as a first-party system.

Infrastructure providers used to operate this system may process limited technical information necessary to receive requests, maintain security, and operate the service.

These providers are not authorized to use Viisi's analytics information for advertising or independent behavioral profiling.

Viisi does not integrate third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Viisi is designed to be suitable for players of all ages.

The game does not require users to create accounts or provide personal contact information.

Viisi does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its gameplay analytics or use their information for advertising or profiling.

Parents and guardians should supervise children when they use optional features that communicate with external services, such as email or sharing.

If you believe personal information has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the request.

Data Security and Retention

Viisi uses data-minimization practices intended to protect player privacy.

  • Analytics information is transmitted using encrypted connections.
  • Access to analytics administration is restricted.
  • Gameplay statistics are maintained primarily as aggregate measurements.
  • Temporary information used to prevent duplicate counting is deleted automatically.
  • Analytics are kept separate from personal iCloud synchronization data.
  • We do not maintain persistent individual gameplay profiles.

Aggregate statistics may be retained to understand long-term game performance and improve future versions of Viisi.

Technical service providers may process limited connection information as necessary for security and service operation, subject to their applicable retention practices.

Your Privacy Choices

You can play Viisi without creating an account or signing in to Game Center.

You can also:

  • Play without an internet connection.
  • Disable iCloud synchronization through your device settings.
  • Choose whether to use Game Center.
  • Avoid optional sharing and support email features.
  • Manage Apple's diagnostic and analytics sharing preferences through iOS settings.

Because Viisi's gameplay analytics are designed to avoid persistent individual identities, aggregate statistics cannot generally be retrieved, modified, or deleted for a specific player.

If you have questions about information you have voluntarily provided through support communications, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Viisi's features, services, or data practices change.

Material changes to data collection or processing will be reflected in this policy.

The current version will always be available on the official Viisi website.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Viisi's privacy practices, please contact:

Email: Support@Viisi.app

Website: https://viisi.app

We are committed to keeping Viisi enjoyable, transparent, and respectful of your privacy.
