How to play Viisi

Viisi is a mindful daily puzzle. You receive five letters and must craft five different words using all of them. Every player worldwide solves the same board each day, so your streak is part of a shared ritual.

Watch Demo

The Basics

Simple rules keep Viisi approachable while still offering satisfying depth.

  1. Use every letter once per word.
  2. Words must be in the official Viisi dictionary—no abbreviations or names.
  3. Each row holds one word. Lock it in to move to the next row—five locked rows wins the day.
  4. Leaving the game mid-row clears the partially typed words. Commit before you wander!
  5. Some puzzles hide bonus words. Find one and its tiles turn gold for extra points.
  6. Level-up your vocabulary! After you win a game click the words to reveal definitions.
  7. Hungry for more games? Go to the archive and play games in the past.
  8. A new puzzle unlocks every day at 06:00 UTC, the same board for every player worldwide.

Helpful Tools

Use these options if you feel stuck—your stats will note when hints are involved.

💡

Hints

Lock in three words and the hint button lights up. Tap it to clear your current row and prefill two letters of a solution. Two hints per puzzle, 10 points each, and a hint never points at a bonus word.

🔍

Reveal

Ready to surrender? Reveal uncovers every answer in the current puzzle. There is no undo—the day counts as played but not solved, so your streak ends.

♻️

Reset

Replay any puzzle from a clean board. Your original score, time, and stats are frozen and never change—a replay is just for fun.

Expert Mode

Turn it on in Settings when the daily board stops fighting back. It becomes the default for new games.

⚡️

No hints

Hints are switched off entirely. It is you, five letters, and the dictionary.

❌

Three strikes

Three invalid submissions end the game. Think before you lock in a word.

🔒

Locked in

A puzzle keeps the mode it started in, so switching the setting never changes a game already in progress.
