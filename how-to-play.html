Your first Viisi puzzle

How to play Viisi. Find your five.

One set of letters. Five different words. Learn the essentials, then settle into your new daily word ritual.

01 / The basics

Same letters.
New possibilities.

  1. 1

    Start with five letters.

    Open the daily puzzle and study your letters. Every player worldwide gets the same board.

  2. 2

    Rearrange every letter.

    Use all five letters exactly once per word. Words must be accepted by Viisi’s dictionary; proper names and abbreviations do not count.

  3. 3

    Find five different words.

    A word cannot count twice. There can be more than five answers, so any five different accepted words can complete the puzzle.

After a win, tap any completed word to see its definition.

A five-letter anagram example

Anagrams are different words made by rearranging the same letters.

The example uses the letters A, E, L, R, and T.

AELRT
  • ALERT1 of 5
  • ALTER2 of 5
  • LATER3 of 5

Same letters, rearranged into three different words. Find two more accepted words to win. This illustrates the rules, not today’s puzzle.

06:00 UTC

A fresh challenge, every day.

A new shared puzzle starts at 06:00 UTC. Your local time depends on your time zone and daylight saving time; the app shows a countdown.

02 / Your options

A little help.
Or an extra challenge.

Choose Classic or Expert before you start. Your mode locks once play begins and stays with that attempt.

Room to explore

Classic Mode

Work through the puzzle with hints available after you find three words. You can use up to two, at 10 points each. Invalid submissions do not have Expert Mode’s three-attempt limit.

Raise the stakes

Expert Mode

No hints. Three invalid submissions end the attempt. Think before you submit, and earn 30 extra points when you complete the puzzle.

How do hints work?

In Classic Mode, hints unlock after three words. A hint clears the current row and fills the first two letters of a solution. You can use up to two hints, at 10 points each. Hints never reveal a Special Word, and your stats note when you use them.

What happens if I reveal the solution?

Reveal shows the solution and ends the attempt without a win. There is no undo. Revealing today’s puzzle also ends the current streak.

Can I practice or replay a puzzle?

The in-app Tutorial lets you practice without affecting your results or statistics. Replay it any time from How to Play in the app.

Reset / replay lets you play a finished puzzle for practice. Your original result, score, time, and statistics stay unchanged, and replays do not add to Total Score. You can also try the one-puzzle browser demo.

Can I play a day I missed?

Open the Archive to find past puzzles. A first-time archive win counts as a win, but it does not extend your daily streak.

Any tips for finding more anagrams?
  • Move the vowels. Try them in different positions instead of keeping the same word shape.
  • Try a different ending. If the letters allow it, look for endings such as -ER or -S, then rearrange the remaining letters.
  • Check every letter. A familiar word only works if it uses every supplied letter exactly once and is accepted by Viisi’s dictionary.

03 / Scores & progress

Every solve adds up.

Earn points, keep your daily streak going, and celebrate the little milestones along the way.

Your progress, at a glance.

Scores track each win. Badges mark your milestones. Supported progress can sync through iCloud across devices using the same Apple ID when available.

Which points count toward Total Score?

Points appear as you complete words, even before you finish. Points from an unsuccessful attempt may still be displayed, but they do not add to Total Score.

Only eligible original wins count, once per puzzle. Reveals, Expert fail-outs, and practice replays do not add to that total.

What do my stats mean?
Wins
Total puzzles solved across daily and archive play.
Win Rate
The share of puzzles you finished that ended in a solve.
Streak
Consecutive daily wins. Archive catch-ups do not extend it.
Performance
Classic and Expert breakdowns, fastest and average solve times, and hints used.
Leaderboards
Compare supported results through Game Center when available.
Which badges can I earn?
