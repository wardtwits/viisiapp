How to play Viisi

Viisi is a mindful daily puzzle. You receive five letters and must craft five different words using all of them. Every player worldwide solves the same board each day, so your streak is part of a shared ritual.

Watch Demo

The Basics

Simple rules keep Viisi approachable while still offering satisfying depth.

  1. Open today’s puzzle and study the five letters.
  2. Arrange all five letters to make a word. Each letter must be used exactly once.
  3. Keep finding different accepted words. A word cannot count twice, and proper names or abbreviations are not accepted.
  4. Find five words to win. Some puzzles also hide Special Words that turn gold and earn extra points.
  5. A new puzzle unlocks every day at 06:00 UTC, the same board for every player worldwide.
  6. After you win, tap any completed word to see its definition.

A five-letter anagram example

An anagram is a word made by rearranging the letters of another word. Viisi asks you to find several words from the same five letters.

Take the letters A, E, L, R, T. You can arrange them as ALERT, move the letters to make ALTER, then rearrange them again to make LATER. Each word uses every letter exactly once.

Those are three anagrams, so you would still need two more different accepted words to win. This is an illustration of the rules, not today’s puzzle. Try the one-puzzle browser demo to practice on a complete board.

Tips for finding more anagrams

  • Move the vowels. Try placing them in different positions instead of keeping the same word shape.
  • Try a different ending. If the letters allow it, look for endings such as -ER or -S, then rearrange the remaining letters.
  • Check every letter. A familiar word only works if it uses all five supplied letters exactly once and is accepted by Viisi’s dictionary.

Helpful Tools

Use these options if you feel stuck—your stats will note when hints are involved. Want more puzzles? The archive has every past day.

▶️

Tutorial

Practice the board without affecting your results or statistics. Replay it any time from How to Play in the app.

💡

Hints

After three words, a hint clears the current row and fills the first two letters of a solution. Two hints per puzzle, 10 points each, and never a Special Word.

🔍

Reveal

Shows the solution and ends the attempt without a win. There is no undo, and revealing today’s puzzle ends the current streak.

♻️

Reset / replay

Replay a puzzle for practice. The original result and statistics do not change, and a replay does not add to Total Score.

🗂️

Archive

Play past puzzles you missed. A first-time archive win counts as a win, but it does not extend your daily streak.

Expert Mode

Choose Expert Mode before starting a puzzle. Once play begins, the mode is locked for that attempt.

⚡️ No hints Hints are switched off entirely. It is you, five letters, and the dictionary.
❌ Three strikes Three invalid submissions end the game. Think before you lock in a word.
🔒 Locked in A puzzle keeps the mode it started in, so switching the setting never changes a game in progress.

Scoring

Every win is scored. Your score drives your performance stats and the celebration screen breakdown. See the full scoring breakdown.

20 points per word Credited the moment you lock in a correct word—you don't have to finish to start earning.
+20 for finishing All 5 words locked in adds a completion bonus. Expert Mode adds +30 more on top.
+25 per Special Word Each Special Word you submit adds 25 points. Hints will never hand you one.
-10 per hint Each hinted row costs 10 points. Skip both for a clean sheet.

Points from an unsuccessful attempt may still be displayed, but they do not add to Total Score. Only eligible original wins count toward that total.

Badges & Stats

Badges mark your milestones. Supported progress can sync through iCloud across devices using the same Apple ID when available.

5 Streak

Solve 5 daily puzzles in 5 consecutive days (archive doesn't count).

25 Wins

Reach twenty-five lifetime Viisi victories.

Hint-Free

Win a puzzle without using a single hint.

One Shot

Solve a puzzle on your very first attempt—without leaving the game board. No resets or reveals.
